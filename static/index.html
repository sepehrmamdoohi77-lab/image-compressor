Online file compression for images and videos

Use this online image compressor and video compressor to reduce file sizes in your browser workflow. Image compression supports JPG, PNG, WEBP, GIF, BMP, TIFF, HEIC and AVIF, while video compression supports MP4, WEBM, MOV, MKV and AVI. Adjust the compression strength to find a practical balance between quality and size.

Compressing files online can make uploads faster and sharing easier, and can help prepare media for websites, stores, email and social platforms. You can reduce the size of an image or video without installing additional software, then download the compressed result for web use or sharing.

Frequently asked questions

What image formats can I compress?

You can upload JPG, PNG, WEBP, GIF, BMP, TIFF, HEIC and AVIF files, then choose an available output format.

What video formats are supported?

The video tool accepts common formats including MP4, WEBM, MOV, MKV, AVI, MPEG, WMV, 3GP and M2TS. MP4 and WEBM are available as practical output choices.

Will compression reduce image or video quality?

Higher compression usually creates a smaller file and may reduce quality. Use the strength control to choose a balance that works for your intended web or sharing use.

What are the file size limits?

Images can be up to 100 MB and videos can be up to 1 GB, subject to the limits shown by the service.

How do I compress a file?

Choose or drop an image or video, select an output format and compression strength, then select the relevant compress button to download the result.
